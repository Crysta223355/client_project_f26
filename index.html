Welcome to the UMSI Engaged Learning Office

Build Skills. Work with Partners. Have an Impact.

The UMSI Engaged Learning Office connects students with opportunities to apply classroom knowledge to real challenges. Use this site to prepare for your experience, begin a strong partnership, and navigate your work.

Why Use This Resource?

Real-world projects rarely follow a perfect plan. This site helps you find guidance when you need it, so you can spend less time searching for materials and more time learning, collaborating, and moving your project forward.

  • Follow a clear process: Move from preparation to project launch and active work.
  • Find practical support: Access useful questions, checklists, and project resources.
  • Build professional skills: Strengthen communication, teamwork, project management, and ethical decision-making.
  • Stay connected to partner needs: Use feedback and reflection to keep the work useful.
