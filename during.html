During Your Experience

Keep the Project Moving Together

Strong engaged learning work depends on communication, shared ownership, and a willingness to adjust. Use regular check-ins to make progress visible, raise concerns early, and keep decisions connected to partner priorities.

Build a Healthy Working Rhythm

Manage the Work

  • Break deliverables into clear tasks with owners and realistic due dates.
  • Record decisions, open questions, and next steps in one shared location.
  • Review risks and dependencies before they affect the schedule.
  • Revisit the scope when new information changes what is feasible or useful.

Communicate with Your Partner

Send an agenda before meetings and a short summary afterward. Explain progress in plain language, identify the feedback you need, and confirm who will make each decision.

When plans change, describe the reason, impact, and available options early so the partner has time to help shape the response.

Use Feedback Productively

  1. Listen for the need behind the comment.
  2. Ask clarifying questions before proposing a response.
  3. Group feedback into required changes, questions, and future ideas.
  4. Document what the team will change and what remains outside the scope.
  5. Follow up so the partner can see how their input affected the work.

Respond to Challenges

Unexpected constraints are normal. When a problem appears, describe it clearly, gather the information needed to understand it, and discuss possible responses before changing an important commitment.

Pause and Ask for Support When

  • A request falls outside the agreed project scope.
  • The team is unsure whether it may collect, store, or share information.
  • A conflict prevents team members or partners from participating fully.
  • A deadline creates pressure to skip accessibility, consent, or quality checks.

Contact your instructor or the Engaged Learning Office before making a high-impact decision when the right approach is unclear.

Quick Weekly Check-In

  • What did we complete?
  • What did we learn?
  • What is blocked?
  • What decision or feedback do we need next?
